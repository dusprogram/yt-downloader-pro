Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2025

Information We Collect

YT Downloader Pro is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information necessary to provide our service:

  • YouTube URLs: Only temporarily processed for download purposes
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics via Netlify Analytics
  • Technical Data: Basic browser information for compatibility

How We Use Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Processing video download requests
  • Improving service performance and reliability
  • Ensuring security and preventing abuse

We do not: Store, track, or share personal information or download history.

Data Storage and Security

Your privacy is our priority:

  • No Permanent Storage: Downloaded files are automatically deleted within 2 minutes
  • No User Accounts: No registration or personal information required
  • Secure Connections: All communications use HTTPS encryption
  • No Tracking: We don't use tracking pixels, cookies, or analytics that identify users

Third-Party Services

Our service utilizes:

  • yt-dlp: Open-source YouTube downloading tool
  • Netlify: Frontend hosting with basic analytics
  • Railway: Backend hosting for processing

These services may have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review.

Your Rights

Since we don't store personal information, there's nothing to delete or modify. However, you have the right to:

  • Use our service anonymously
  • Contact us with privacy concerns
  • Stop using our service at any time

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you're a parent and believe your child has used our service, please contact us.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of our service after changes constitutes acceptance.
